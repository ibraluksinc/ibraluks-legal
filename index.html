Ibraluks Rides

Ibraluks Rides is a software platform that connects riders with independent drivers. Riders use the app to request a ride from a nearby independent driver, see an upfront fare estimate, and track the trip; independent drivers use it to go online when they choose, accept requests, navigate, and get paid.
